This reputation—which makes us a trusted household brand by 2030—will result from the realization of our strategic aspirations and will promote our continuing efforts to help those we serve feel whole.

AdventHealth System — metrics that move together

Reading across 55 facilities under AdventHealth System, this looks for Vision 2030 metrics whose facility-level scores rise and fall together — a signal they may share a common driver or trade off against one another.

How this is computed. Each metric’s status is scored on the workbook scale (at goal = 100, approaching = 73, below = 30) and a Pearson correlation (r) is run for every pair of metrics across the 55 facilities. Only pairs where both metrics vary and share at least five reporting facilities appear; |r| ≥ 0.4 is flagged notable (≥ 0.7 strong, ≥ 0.5 moderate). Correlation is not causation — these are prompts to investigate, not proof.
